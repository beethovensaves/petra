# M1.8 bounded Felicity revision

Status: semantic pass for `M1.8-EV09`; `M1.8-W14` in progress

Scope: deterministic language cleanup and post-rewrite preservation checks

Date: 2026-09-04

## Outcome

Petra now has a bounded, model-free Felicity revision engine. It creates a new
immutable DraftArtifact revision rather than changing the calibrated source
draft. Version 1.0 removes only three allowlisted filler openings and normalizes
horizontal whitespace. It does not paraphrase, reorder, split, merge, or invent
scientific language.

The output is service-produced with `edit_origin: felicity_revision`. Every
DraftSpan receives a new identity and Unicode code-point range. Scientific
force, semantic origin, materiality, provenance bases, canonical WorkVersion
scope, source-access depth, and citation placement are carried forward exactly.
The pending Felicity warning is replaced by five explicit passing validators:

- Felicity language;
- scientific-force preservation;
- epistemic-origin preservation;
- provenance-basis preservation;
- citation-scope preservation.

## Fail-closed language boundary

The initial deterministic validator rejects proof, confirmation, disproof,
certainty, and `clearly` markers because the current composition profile has no
formal domain-specific permission for those terms. A direct source report must
retain visible attribution to authors, researchers, a study, paper, or source.
A Petra inference must retain an explicit inference or uncertainty marker.

These checks are intentionally conservative and incomplete as a general prose
editor. They establish a useful safe floor. A later model-powered Felicity role
may propose more natural revisions only after it has its own role contract,
certification suite, execution record, and the same post-rewrite preservation
gate. Model fluency will not weaken this deterministic boundary.

## Visible receipt

`FelicityRevisionRecord@1.0.0` binds the command, exact previous and revised
DraftArtifact revisions, language contract, ProvenanceMap, CitationBundle,
exhaustive one-to-one DraftSpan transformations, before-and-after text hashes,
provenance-basis snapshots, citation links, preservation results, responsible
service, and time. It explicitly cannot become scientific evidence.

One accepted and one rejected fixture pass through JSON Schema, generated
TypeScript, and strict Pydantic parity. Four core cases prove deterministic
replay, additive revision, allowlisted filler removal, preservation of support
and citation scope, proof-language refusal, attribution refusal, immutable
receipt replay, and tamper rejection.

The complete repository gate passes with 599 always-on tests and seven expected
service-dependent skips.

## Remaining boundary

The next increment will persist the FelicityRevisionRecord and its complete
DraftArtifact revision atomically, retain exact command replay, enforce the
required validator set in PostgreSQL, and prove restart rehydration without
reopening the broader model-generated rewrite problem.
