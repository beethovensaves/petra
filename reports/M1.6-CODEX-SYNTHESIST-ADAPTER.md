## Authentication boundary

- Petra starts `codex app-server` as a sidecar and communicates through its JSON-RPC interface.
- The adapter requires `account/read` to report an authenticated `chatgpt` session.
- An API-key-authenticated Codex session is rejected rather than used as a fallback.
- Codex owns OAuth persistence and refresh; Petra neither reads nor copies credential files.
- The adapter accepts no API-key option and sends no credential material in its thread or turn requests.

## Scientific boundary

The turn is ephemeral, read-only, and configured with `approvalPolicy: "never"`. Tool activity, browsing, commands, file access, MCP activity, delegation, malformed output, oversized output, timeouts, and unsupported sampling controls invalidate the result.

This live pass proves the transport, OAuth path, structured-output projection, and deterministic grounding gate. It is not yet a synthesist role certification. Petra will activate a deployment for routine synthesis only after the exact prompt, schema projection, packet fixture suite, and validators pass the separate frozen certification process.
