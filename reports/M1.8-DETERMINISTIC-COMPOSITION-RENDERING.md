# M1.8 deterministic composition rendering

Status: pass for `M1.8-EV05`; `M1.8-W07` and `M1.8-W08` in progress

Scope: portable RenderedArtifact contract and model-free Markdown, LaTeX, and BibTeX rendering

Date: 2026-09-04

## Outcome

Petra can now transform one immutable scientific composition aggregate into
Markdown, a standalone LaTeX document, and BibTeX without asking a model to
rewrite the draft. Every output is an exact UTF-8 byte sequence and a valid,
content-addressed Petra Artifact candidate.

The new `RenderedArtifact@1.0.0` contract records the exact DraftArtifact and
CitationBundle inputs, renderer profile and hash, output Artifact identity,
render options, visible warnings, transformations, semantic content hash, and
service actor. It explicitly states that a rendered representation is not
scientific authority and cannot change support.

## Rendering behavior

- Citations are inserted only after their linked DraftSpans. They are never
  assigned by text similarity or formatter inference.
- Markdown adds linked bibliography keys and a canonical References section.
- LaTeX escapes control characters, retains Unicode text, inserts `\cite{}`
  commands, and emits a self-contained `thebibliography` document.
- BibTeX uses the canonical bibliography key, Work kind, exact display names,
  available date precision, and verified identifiers.
- Display names are preserved as supplied; the renderer does not guess family
  names, initials, or organizational authorship.
- Missing year, identifier, journal, volume, issue, or page data stays absent.
  Petra does not manufacture fields to make a citation look complete.
- Provisional or disputed identity, incomplete metadata, retraction, and
  abstract-only access remain explicit warnings in both the rendered bytes and
  the RenderedArtifact record.
- Every transformation records whether it is lossy and fixes its semantic
  effect to `none`.

## Executable evidence

Three accepted RenderedArtifact fixtures cover Markdown, LaTeX, and BibTeX in
Ajv 2020 and strict Pydantic parity; a rejected fixture proves the renderer
cannot declare scientific authority. The contract suite passes 272 cases.

Five renderer cases prove deterministic byte and Artifact identity, exact
DraftArtifact and CitationBundle binding, linked-span citation insertion,
visible warnings, omission of unknown bibliography fields, UTF-8 and LaTeX
escaping, format authorization, and failure on aggregate drift. The focused
compiler and renderer suite passes all ten cases.

The complete repository verification gate passes with 581 always-on tests and
seven expected service-dependent skips. That total includes 272 contract and
204 core cases, alongside the adapter, retrieval, server, storage, graph, and
Python suites.

## Remaining boundary

The renderer currently returns verified contract records, Artifact candidates,
and exact bytes. `M1.8-W07` and `M1.8-W08` remain in progress until the bytes are
committed to Petra's content-addressed ArtifactStore and the RenderedArtifact
records are durably linked to the source aggregate in PostgreSQL. That is the
next increment.
