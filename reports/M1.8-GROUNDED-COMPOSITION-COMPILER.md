# M1.8 grounded composition compiler

Status: pass for `M1.8-EV03`; `M1.8-W04`–`M1.8-W06` remain in progress

Scope: deterministic DraftArtifact, DraftSpan, ProvenanceMap, and CitationBundle compilation

Date: 2026-09-04

## Outcome

Petra can now compile its first complete scientific composition aggregate from
an exact CompositionRequest, ready CompositionPlan, EvidencePacket, completed
GroundedResponse, and canonical scholarly records.

The compiler does not scrape fluent response text and guess where its support
came from. It concatenates the plan's ordered, inspectable language moves and
turns each move into one Unicode-code-point DraftSpan. The move's separately
recorded origin, scientific force, evidence references, citation obligation,
and limitations become the draft's provenance and validation inputs.

Draft, span, provenance, citation, and bibliography identities are derived
deterministically from the immutable plan and move identities. Exact recompilation
therefore produces the same aggregate without clock- or provider-dependent IDs.

## Evidence and citation behavior

- Source-report spans reproduce exact EvidencePacket selections in their
  ProvenanceMap bases.
- Petra inference, researcher language, and structural language have distinct
  provenance basis types; inference is not mislabeled as scientific force.
- Citation-required moves must traverse their selected ContextPacket records
  to at least one canonical WorkVersion.
- Citation metadata, authors, identifiers, publication status, version, and
  visible provisional/disputed warnings are derived only from canonical
  scholarly records.
- Citation source access and SourceSpan locators cannot exceed the source depth
  carried by the selected context.
- Metadata-only or non-source context cannot produce material scientific prose.
- Abstract-only evidence may produce only broad source-reporting force. It is
  explicitly labeled in draft limitations; causal, mechanistic, and
  methodological force is rejected.
