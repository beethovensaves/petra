# M1.6 packet compilation

**Status:** pass for `M1.6-W06`–`M1.6-W12`  
**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Scope:** deterministic ContextPacket and composition-specific EvidencePacket compilation

## Outcome

Petra can now turn bounded retrieval selections into immutable packet records
without allowing a graph projection or model to manufacture scientific state.
`ContextPacketCompiler` accepts canonical Claim, Assertion, SourceSpan, or
WorkVersion records, computes their exact refs and token estimates, assembles a
bounded packet, and registers it through the existing invariant-checking
`InMemoryContextPacketRegistry`.

It then compiles an exhaustive, composition-specific EvidencePacket over the
registered context. The EvidencePacket retains the exact ContextPacket content
hash and snapshot and records selected evidence, exclusions, bearing, source
locations, coverage, temporal perspective, and freshness.

## Scientific and authority guarantees

- Project, graph snapshot, ContextRecipe revision, objective, target refs, and
  dependency hashes are part of the immutable ContextPacket identity.
- Object refs and trust/review metadata are derived from exact canonical
  records. Retrieval projections cannot supply or upgrade them.
- A source-backed packet must include a content-hashed `rights-decision:` policy
  dependency. Source access separately records metadata-only, abstract-only,
  structured-full-text, or analyzed-full-text depth.
- Claims and Assertions bring every named SourceSpan into the same selection
  closure. A target and its required source locations are indivisible under
  budget pressure.
- Non-target candidates that do not fit remain typed, material budget
  omissions. Coverage therefore cannot silently appear complete.
- Evidence compilation partitions every context record into selected or
  excluded state and records supporting, contrary, qualifying, conflicting,
  background, or unresolved bearing.
- Selected Claims and Assertions cannot outlive or hide an excluded cited
  SourceSpan.

## Budget and identity

The first estimator is deterministic and deliberately simple: UTF-8 bytes of
canonical JSON divided by four, rounded up. It is an inspectable input-budget
estimate, not a promise about a provider tokenizer. The packet records both the
limit and exact estimated use. A later model-specific compiler may add a pinned
tokenizer dependency without changing the packet authority boundary.

ContextPacket and EvidencePacket IDs are derived from their complete immutable
pre-ID records. Exact retries therefore return the already frozen registry
record; changed content, snapshot, compilation time, or dependency identity
produces a different ID.

## Verification

Six compiler tests prove:

1. immutable, content-hashed ContextPacket compilation and exact replay;
2. provenance-preserving budget truncation with a visible material omission;
3. fail-closed target and SourceSpan closure;
4. a required hashed rights-decision dependency;
5. exhaustive composition-specific EvidencePacket compilation; and
6. distinct contrary and qualifying coverage plus refusal of a hidden source
   exclusion.

The complete repository gate passes with 235 contract, 164 core, 26 adapter,
15 retrieval, two always-on Neo4j, two always-on S3, 16 server, and 25 Python
tests: 485 always-on tests total, with six expected external-service skips.

## Deliberate limits and next slice

This compiler does not treat a graph property view as a canonical record,
persist packets to PostgreSQL, establish literature-wide coverage, measure a
provider's exact tokenization, or generate scientific prose. The M1 fixture uses
one source and therefore requires at least one packet-level rights-decision
dependency; a future multi-source compiler should bind each included WorkVersion
or Artifact to its exact RightsDecision explicitly if the packet schema is
extended.

The next slice is `M1.6-W13`–`M1.6-W16`: define and certify a synthesist output
contract, run one bounded answer through an eligible deployment, validate every
material statement against the EvidencePacket and SourceSpans, and preserve the
distinction between source report, Petra synthesis, and unresolved inference.
