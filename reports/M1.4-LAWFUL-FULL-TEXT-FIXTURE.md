## Scientific and rights boundary

The paper is a peer-reviewed journal Comment, not an empirical experiment. That
is acceptable because this fixture tests the ingestion contract rather than
selecting Petra's flagship research question. The PDF combines prose,
multi-column reading order, boxed definitions, and a hierarchical principles
box in a small, stable artifact.

The exact PDF carries a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 notice. Petra's
copy-specific decision permits storage, local transformation, indexing,
attributed quotation, and redistribution. It separately disables remote-model
egress. This proves that source rights and runtime capability are intersected,
not treated as synonyms.

The source judgments identify:

- a broad statement about machine discovery and use of data;
- a local qualification that the principles are not themselves a standard or
  specification;
- Box 1, Box 2, and multi-column body prose as layout targets.

The addendum relationship is recorded without pretending it overwrote the
acquired bytes.
